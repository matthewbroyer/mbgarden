# Attributions — mbgarden.online

Last updated: October 13, 2026

## Gardening data
The numbers and guidance in the app are facts from the public sources below, restated in the app's own words. No text or tables were copied. Each crop card links its sources, each life-cycle line links the specific source it came from, and all sources are listed on the in-app Credits page and in Help.

The growing and harvest tips were written for this app as general guidance. The Feeding lines follow the app's own standardized plan (worm castings, an all-purpose feed, a bloom formula) and do not come from these sources.

### Spacing
- Square Foot Gardening Foundation: spacing chart. https://squarefootgardening.org/2024/02/square-foot-spacing/
- Phipps Conservatory: Square Foot Gardening Guide (PDF). https://www.phipps.conservatory.org/assets/documents/Square_Foot_Gardening_Guide_2020.pdf
- Garden In Minutes: SFG plant spacing chart. https://gardeninminutes.com/blogs/easy-growing/plant-spacing-chart-square-foot-gardening

### Planting dates and zones
- UNH Extension: When to Plant Your Vegetable Garden. https://extension.unh.edu/resource/growing-vegetables-when-plant-your-vegetable-garden-fact-sheet
- UNH Extension: Cool Season Vegetables. https://extension.unh.edu/blog/2019/04/cool-season-vegetables
- University of Minnesota Extension: Planting and growing guides. https://extension.umn.edu/how/planting-and-growing-guides
- USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map. https://planthardiness.ars.usda.gov/
- Old Farmer's Almanac: frost dates by ZIP (linked only). https://www.almanac.com/gardening/frostdates

The per-zone frost-date defaults are approximate averages compiled for this app. The app tells users to enter their own local dates.

### Seed starting and germination
- Oregon State University Extension: Soil temperature conditions for vegetable seed germination (data from J.F. Harrington, UC Davis). https://extension.oregonstate.edu/gardening/soil-compost/soil-temperature-conditions-vegetable-seed-germination
- UC Master Gardener Program (UC ANR): Seed germination temperature and timing (Harrington data, as printed in the *California Master Gardener Handbook*). Used for okra, celery and leek. https://ucanr.edu/program/uc-master-gardener-program/seed-germination-temperature-and-timing
- Colorado State University Extension, CMG GardenNotes #720: Vegetable Planting Guide (seed depth, days to germinate). https://cmg.extension.colostate.edu/Gardennotes/720.pdf
- University of Maine Cooperative Extension, Bulletin #2751: Starting Seeds at Home (Peronto and Guethler). Covers the heat mat, humidity dome, lights, hardening off, transplant timing and the seed-depth rule of thumb. https://extension.umaine.edu/publications/2751e/

### Independence
mbgarden.online is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the organizations above. Their names are used only to credit sources. The Terms page says the same.

## Fonts
The app uses the visitor's own system fonts (for example Segoe UI on Windows or San Francisco on Apple devices). It doesn't embed or download any fonts, and the standalone build's security policy blocks font loading (`font-src 'none'`). The earlier embedded OFL fonts (Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono) have been removed, so no font license applies.

## Emoji and icons
The crop emoji are Unicode characters drawn by the visitor's own operating-system emoji font. No emoji images are bundled. The mbgarden.online logo (a leaf over a soil row, on the MB Suite gradient badge) is an original SVG made for this app in October 2026. The app icons, favicon and `og-image.png` are rendered from it. The paper-cutout sprouts on the Today banner, share cards and OG image (template NA-17, "Garden Rows") are original artwork from the MB Suite nature-shape set.

## Code
There are no third-party JavaScript or CSS libraries. All code is original. The MB Suite scripts (tab titles, Install card, share cards) and CSS layers inside `index.html` come from Matthew Broyer's own MB Suite design kit, shared by his apps.
