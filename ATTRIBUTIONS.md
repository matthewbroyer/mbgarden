## Gardening data
The spacing and timing numbers are facts collected from the public sources below. They are credited in the app on every crop card, in Help and on the Credits page. The growing and harvest tips were written for this app; no text was copied from these sources.
- Square Foot Gardening Foundation: spacing chart — https://squarefootgardening.org/2024/02/square-foot-spacing/
- Phipps Conservatory: Square Foot Gardening Guide (PDF) — https://www.phipps.conservatory.org/assets/documents/Square_Foot_Gardening_Guide_2020.pdf
- Garden In Minutes: SFG plant spacing chart — https://gardeninminutes.com/blogs/easy-growing/plant-spacing-chart-square-foot-gardening
- UNH Extension: When to Plant Your Vegetable Garden — https://extension.unh.edu/resource/growing-vegetables-when-plant-your-vegetable-garden-fact-sheet
- UNH Extension: Cool Season Vegetables — https://extension.unh.edu/blog/2019/04/cool-season-vegetables
- University of Minnesota Extension: Planting and growing guides — https://extension.umn.edu/how/planting-and-growing-guides
- USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map — https://planthardiness.ars.usda.gov/
- Old Farmer's Almanac: frost dates by ZIP (linked only) — https://www.almanac.com/gardening/frostdates

The per-zone frost-date defaults are approximate averages compiled for this app. The app tells users to enter their own local dates.

## Emoji and icons
The crop emoji are Unicode characters drawn by the visitor's own operating-system emoji font. No emoji images are bundled. The mbgarden.online logo (four squares on soil) is an original inline SVG.
